Privacy

Hang Eleven has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't ask for your location.

Saved on your device

These stay in your browser's local storage, on this device only. Hang Eleven never sends them anywhere.

  • The spots you mark as favourites.
  • The last forecast downloaded, so it still shows when you're offline. It is replaced each time a new one loads.
  • A Mapbox or MapTiler key, only if you enter one and leave "Remember on this device" ticked.

To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or delete the app from your home screen.

What the app requests from other services

  • Forecast: Open-Meteo, for two fixed points off and on the Saunton coast. Your own location isn't included. Like any web server, Open-Meteo receives your IP address with each request.
  • Code and fonts: the three.js 3D library from the jsDelivr CDN, and typefaces from Google Fonts. These services also receive your IP address.
  • Sharper imagery (optional): if you add a key, map tiles come from the provider you choose (Mapbox or MapTiler), with your key attached.

Each of these services handles requests under its own privacy policy.
