What's right above me?

What’s Up finds you with your phone's GPS and shows the planes, satellites and planets above you. The map covers a 1-mile radius around you, and anything directly over your head lights up yellow.

Plane Satellite Sun, Moon, planet Directly overhead Top is north. Tap anything for details.

Aircraft positions come from community ADS-B networks (adsb.lol, airplanes.live, adsb.fi, with OpenSky as a fallback); not every aircraft broadcasts, and military or private flights may be missing. Satellite orbits come from CelesTrak and are computed on your phone with satellite.js. The Sun, Moon and planets are computed on your phone with Astronomy Engine. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors. Positions are approximate. Don't use this for navigation or safety.

Your location stays on your device except for the rounded coordinates sent to the aircraft service to ask what's nearby.
